# Privacy Policy — tube-autopilot

**Last updated: 2026-09-15**

tube-autopilot is a personal automation tool that publishes short-form videos
to YouTube on behalf of its owner. This policy explains what happens to
information when the app is used.

## 1. What the app does
The app connects to the YouTube account of its owner (the same person who
authorized it) through the official YouTube Data API and OAuth 2.0. It uses
this connection only to:

- upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channels
- read basic public channel statistics (subscriber count, view counts) for
  those same channels

It does **not** read, modify, or store any information about other people.

## 2. What information is handled
- **YouTube account access:** access is granted by the owner via Google's
  OAuth consent screen. The app stores only the OAuth tokens needed to keep
  the connection working. Tokens are kept in private, access-restricted
  storage (GitHub Actions encrypted secrets) and are never shared publicly.
- **Video content:** scripts, voice-over audio, and rendered videos are
  generated by the app itself and uploaded to the owner's channels.
- **No personal data of third parties** is collected, processed, or sold.

## 3. Where data lives
All credentials and generated content live either on the owner's own
infrastructure (their GitHub repository and its encrypted secrets) or on
YouTube itself. The app runs on GitHub's servers using the owner's
repository.

## 4. Third-party services
The app relies on these services, each governed by their own policies:

- Google / YouTube (YouTube Data API, YouTube Terms of Service)
- GitHub (GitHub Actions, for running the automation)
